All your building operations in one place.

FaciliHQ is a building management program made for schools and buildings. AC, lights, keys, tickets, tasks and supplies, all in one program that is easy to use.

What FaciliHQ does

One program for the whole building. Each part is simple on its own, and they all work together.

AC and thermostats

See and control every AC unit and thermostat in the building from one screen. Set schedules and keep every room comfortable, without walking from room to room.

Lights

Turn lights on and off room by room, or put them on a schedule. No more walking the building at night to check what was left on.

Keys and locks

Every room, door, lock, key and core on record in one place. Always know who holds which key and which door it opens.

Tickets and tasks

Something broke? Open a ticket, assign it, and follow it until it is done. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Supplies Schedules Reports

Emails and voice notes become tasks

The AI assistant

Forward an email or send a voice note, and FaciliHQ turns it into a task with the details filled in. You stop copying things by hand, and nothing gets lost in the inbox.

Just talk to the building

We are training our own voice agent that understands Yiddish, English and Hebrew, so you and your team can simply talk, in the language that comes naturally.

The whole building in your pocket

FaciliHQ runs where you are. Check the building, answer a ticket or hand out a key, from your desk or from your phone.
